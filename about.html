About · Jasmine Sayed

A little bit about me.

I'm a product designer who specializes in the hard stuff: complex, technical, and AI products. My work runs from an 11,000-model AI platform at Microsoft CoreAI to a privacy-first identity app I led end to end, and a live accessibility product I designed and coded myself. I work where research, design, and code overlap, and I don't stop at the mockup. I ship the thing.

What I care about most is the hard part: the tradeoffs, the features worth cutting, the moment the research contradicts the obvious answer. I led UXFest, a 140-person design event, end to end, and I bring that same ownership to every product I work on: define the problem, make the call, and ship it.
