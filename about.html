A little bit about me.

Jasmine Sayed

Product Designer · Seattle & San Francisco

I'm a product designer who specializes in the hard stuff: complex, technical, and AI products. My work runs from an 11,000-model AI platform, Microsoft Foundry, with the CoreAI team, to a privacy-first identity app I led the design of, and a live accessibility product I designed and coded myself. I work where research, design, and code overlap. I ship the thing.

What I care about most: the tradeoffs, the features worth cutting, the moment the research contradicts the obvious answer. As President of UXFest I ran a 140+ person UX hackathon end to end, and I bring that same ownership to every product I work on: define the problem, make the call, and ship it.
